Hannah’s Kitchen is a West Virginia-based private chef service offering seasonal, farm-to-table meals tailored to families, intimate gatherings, and special events. Led by Chef Hannah Berry, a longtime Eastern and Northern Virginia resident and passionate local food advocate, the service brings thoughtfully crafted menus to homes throughout Washington, D.C., and nearby communities. Every dish is rooted in the freshest local ingredients—sourced from trusted farms, fishermen, and artisans—and prepared with care, creativity, and a deep love of hospitality. Whether you’re hosting a relaxed backyard brunch or a multi-course dinner party, Hannah’s Kitchen delivers a personalized, elevated dining experience right in the comfort of your home.
